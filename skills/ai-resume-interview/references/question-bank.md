# Question Bank

Interview questions organized by section. All questions are optimized for voice input (under 20 words, one question at a time).

---

## CV Extraction (Stage 0)

### Opening

```
"Got a CV to start from? Paste it, give me a file path, or say 'skip' to start fresh."
```

### Verification

```
"Here's what I extracted. Anything wrong or missing?"
```

### Follow-ups

```
"Is your email still [email]?"
"Any roles or skills I missed?"
"Should I add anything else before we continue?"
```

## Profile Quick-Fire (Stage 1)

Questions under 15 words. One answer, move on.

| `not_looking_for` | "What are you actively avoiding?" | "What's a dealbreaker for you?" |

## Career Narrative (Stage 2)

Slower, reflective questions. Allow pauses.

### Core Narrative

```
"Walk me through the thread connecting your roles."
```

Follow-ups:
```
"What pattern do you see across your career?"
"How did one role lead to the next?"
"What's the through-line?"
```

### Motivation

```
"What kind of work makes you lose track of time?"
```

Follow-ups:
```
"When do you feel most energized at work?"
"What problems do you love solving?"
"What would you do even if you weren't paid?"
```

### Future Vision

```
"Two years from now, what does success look like?"
```

Follow-ups:
```
"What are you building toward?"
"What's the next chapter of your career?"
"Where do you want to be?"
```

## Experience Deep Dive (Stage 3)

For each role, starting with most recent.

### Opening

```
"Let's talk about your time at [Company]. You were there as [Title] from [dates]. The CV says [bullets]. Let's go deeper."
```

### Why Questions

| Field | Primary Question | Alternative |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| `why_joined` | "Why did you join [Company]? The real reason." | "What convinced you to take that job?" |
| `why_left` | "Why did you leave? Real talk." | "What pushed you to move on?" |

### Contribution Questions

| Field | Primary Question | Alternative |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| `actual_contributions` | "What's something you did there that's not on your resume?" | "What did you actually spend your time on?" |
| `proudest_achievement` | "What's your single proudest achievement from that role?" | "What are you most proud of from that job?" |

### Challenge Questions

| Field | Primary Question | Alternative |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| `challenges_faced` | "Hardest part of that role?" | "What was the biggest challenge?" |
| `lessons_learned` | "What did that job teach you?" | "What's the main thing you learned there?" |
| `would_do_differently` | "Looking back, what would you do differently?" | "With hindsight, what would you change?" |

### Third-Person Questions

| Field | Primary Question | Alternative |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| `manager_would_say` | "What would your manager honestly say about you?" | "How would your boss describe you?" |
| `reports_would_say` | "What would your direct reports say about working with you?" | "How would your team describe you as a manager?" |

### Polish Detection Probes

Use when answers sound too rehearsed:

```
"That sounds like the interview answer. What's the real one?"
"If I called your manager right now, would they agree?"
"What's the part you're leaving out?"
"What would a coworker who didn't like you say?"
"Be more specific. Give me an example."
```

### Transition Questions

```
"Next role: [Company]. Ready, or need a break?"
"Let's move on to your time at [Company]."
"What about before that, at [Company]?"
```

## Skills Assessment (Stage 4)

### Opening

```
"Your CV mentions [skills]. Let's rate these honestly."
```

### Rating Context (state once)

```
"Quick reference: 7 means you could pass a technical interview. 1-4 is learning, 5-6 is moderate, 7+ is strong."
```

### Per-Skill Questions

| Field | Primary Question | Alternative |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| `self_rating` | "Where 7 means 'pass a tech interview', rate your [Skill]." | "On a scale of 1-10, how strong is your [Skill]?" |
| `evidence` | "What proves that rating?" | "What have you built with [Skill]?" |
| `honest_notes` | "Any caveats about this skill?" | "What would trip you up about [Skill]?" |
| `years_experience` | "How long have you been using [Skill]?" | "When did you start with [Skill]?" |
| `last_used` | "When did you last use [Skill] seriously?" | "Is [Skill] current for you?" |

### Probing for Honesty

```
"What's a [Skill] problem you couldn't solve alone?"
"What would trip you up in an interview about this?"
"What skills do you claim but feel shaky about?"
"What would make you nervous in a [Skill] coding test?"
```

### Discovery Questions

```
"What skills are missing from your CV?"
"What do you use daily that's not listed?"
"Any tools or technologies you're learning?"
```

### Category Clarification

```
"Would you call [Skill] a language, framework, or tool?"
"Is [Skill] more DevOps or Cloud?"
```

## Gaps & Weaknesses (Stage 5)

### Permission Framing

```
"Now for the honest part. These gaps stay with the AI - they help it represent you accurately, not shared directly with employers. Ready?"
```

### Primary Questions

| Field | Primary Question | Alternative |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| `description` | "What's a common skill in your field you're notably weak at?" | "What's a gap in your skillset?" |
| `why_its_a_gap` | "What makes this a gap for you specifically?" | "Why does this matter?" |
| `interest_in_learning` | "How interested are you in actually fixing this?" | "Is this something you want to improve?" |

### Probing Questions

```
"What feedback have you gotten repeatedly?"
"What's been on your 'should learn' list too long?"
"What types of roles should you probably avoid?"
"What would a critical coworker say about you?"
"What's frustrating about working with you?"
"What have past managers wanted you to improve?"
```

### Gap Type Questions

```
"Is this more of a technical gap or a soft skill?"
"Is this about experience you don't have?"
"Is this domain-specific?"
```

### Interest Probing

```
"Do you actually want to fix this, or are you okay with it?"
"Is this something you're actively working on?"
"Would you take a course to improve this?"
```

## FAQ Generation (Stage 6)

### Introduction

```
"Let's pre-generate some interview answers. I'll draft based on everything you've told me."
```

### Standard Questions

1. **Weakness Question**
```
"Let's tackle 'What's your biggest weakness?' Based on what you've shared..."
[Draft answer]
"How does that sound?"
```

2. **Why Leaving**
```
"How about 'Why are you looking to leave?' Here's a draft..."
[Draft answer]
"Accurate?"
```

3. **Five Years**
```
"For 'Where do you see yourself in 5 years?'..."
[Draft answer]
"Feel right?"
```

4. **Why Hire You**
```
"'Why should we hire you?' Based on your strengths..."
[Draft answer]
"Anything to add?"
```

5. **Salary**
```
"'What are your salary expectations?' Given what you said..."
[Draft answer]
"Good?"
```

6. **Failure Story**
```
"'Tell me about a time you failed.' Based on your experiences..."
[Draft answer]
"Does that capture it?"
```

### Refinement Questions

```
"Want to adjust anything?"
"Too honest or not honest enough?"
"Should I tone it down?"
"Want me to make it more direct?"
"Missing any context?"
```

## Voice-Friendly Guidelines

### Do's

- Keep questions under 20 words
- Ask one thing at a time
- Use conversational openers ("Tell me about...", "Walk me through...")
- Allow silence for thinking
- Use the person's name or company names for context

### Don'ts

- No compound questions ("What was X and how did Y?")
- No jargon-heavy questions
- No leading questions that suggest an answer
- Don't interrupt mid-thought
- Don't rapid-fire questions

### Rephrasing Examples

❌ "Can you tell me about the challenges you faced in your role and how you overcame them and what you learned from the experience?"

✅ "Hardest part of that role?"
(then follow up with "How did you handle it?" and "What did you learn?")

❌ "On a scale of 1-10 where 1 is beginner and 10 is expert and 7 means you could pass a technical interview, how would you rate yourself?"

✅ "Where 7 means 'pass a tech interview', rate your TypeScript."
