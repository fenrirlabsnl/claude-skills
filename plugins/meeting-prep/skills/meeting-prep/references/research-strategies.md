# Meeting Prep Research Strategies

Search patterns, fallbacks, and efficiency tactics for attendee research.

---

## Search Budget Guidelines

Research efficiency matters. Too many searches hit rate limits and slow down prep.

### Budget by Meeting Importance

| Meeting Type | Searches per Person | Total Cap |
|--------------|---------------------|-----------|
| Board/Investor meeting | 4-5 | 15 |
| Key client/prospect | 3-4 | 12 |
| Standard external | 2 | 6 |
| Quick intro | 1 | 3 |
| Internal meeting | 0 | 0 |

### Allocation Strategy

1. **Prioritize decision makers** - Research VPs/Directors/C-level more than others
2. **Skip internal attendees** - You know your coworkers
3. **Batch company research** - One search covers multiple attendees from same company
4. **Use LinkedIn efficiently** - One search often provides title, background, connections

## Primary Search Patterns

### Person Lookup (Most Common)

**Pattern:** `"[Full Name]" [Company] LinkedIn`

**Example:** `"Sarah Chen" Acme Corp LinkedIn`

**What you get:**
- LinkedIn profile (title, experience, education)
- Company confirmation
- Shared connections (sometimes)

**Fallback if no results:** Try without quotes, try company domain

### Company Lookup

**Pattern:** `[Company] about` or `site:[domain] about`

**Example:** `Acme Corp about` or `site:acmecorp.com about`

**What you get:**
- Company description
- Industry
- Size indicators
- Recent news in snippets

### Person + News/Speaking

**Pattern:** `"[Full Name]" [Company] interview OR podcast OR speaking`

**Use when:** High-stakes meetings where extra context helps

**What you get:**
- Speaking appearances
- Interview quotes
- Thought leadership topics

## Fallback Strategies

### When LinkedIn Profile Not Found

1. **Try company website team page**
   - `site:[domain] team` or `site:[domain] about`
   - Look for leadership/about pages

2. **Try broader search**
   - `"[Full Name]" [Company]` without "LinkedIn"
   - May find company bio, press releases

3. **Try email domain**
   - Company website usually has /about or /team

4. **Note the gap**
   - "Limited information available"
   - Suggest asking mutual connections

### When Company Unknown

1. **Parse email domain**
   - user@company.com → search "company.com"

2. **Check for LinkedIn company page**
   - `[Domain] LinkedIn company`

3. **Assume startup/small**
   - No online presence often = early stage or small

### When Person is New/Junior

1. **Spend less time**
   - One search max
   - Focus on title/role confirmation

2. **Research their manager instead**
   - Often more publicly available

## Information Extraction

### From LinkedIn Profile

| Look For | Where to Find |
|----------|---------------|
| Current title | Headline or Experience section |
| Time in role | Experience dates |
| Previous companies | Experience history |
| Education | Education section |
| Location | Profile header |
| Mutual connections | "Mutual connections" section |
| Shared groups | Groups section |

### From Company Website

| Look For | Where to Find |
|----------|---------------|
| What they do | Homepage, About page |
| Company size | About page, Careers (job count) |
| Recent news | Blog, Press/News section |
| Team info | Team, Leadership, About pages |
| Funding | About page (sometimes), press releases |

### From Search Snippets

Sometimes you don't need to click through:

- Job title often appears in LinkedIn snippet
- Company description in Google snippet
- Recent news in news tab

---

## Rate Limit Avoidance

### Do

- Batch searches (plan all searches, execute in sequence)
- Use specific queries (fewer refinement searches needed)
- Extract maximum info per search (read snippets, multiple results)
- Cache results mentally (don't re-search same company)

### Don't

- Search for each attendee individually if same company
- Refine searches iteratively (get it right first time)
- Search for information you don't need
- Do deep research for low-stakes meetings

## Quick Research Checklist

For each external attendee:

- [ ] Current title confirmed
- [ ] Company confirmed
- [ ] Time in current role (if visible)
- [ ] One interesting background fact
- [ ] Shared connections (if any)

For the company (once per company):

- [ ] What they do (one sentence)
- [ ] Approximate size
- [ ] Industry
- [ ] Any recent news

---

## Research Output Format

Capture findings in this structure:

```
### [Name] - [Title]
**Company:** [Company] | [Industry] | [Size]
**LinkedIn:** [URL]

**Background:**
- [Key career point]
- [Relevant experience]
- [Education if notable]

**Shared context:**
- [Mutual connections]
- [Shared background]

**Key thing to know:** [One-liner most important fact]
```

## Time Estimates

| Research Task | Time |
|---------------|------|
| Full attendee research (3-4 searches) | 3-5 min |
| Standard attendee (2 searches) | 2-3 min |
| Company research | 1-2 min |
| Email history review | 1-2 min |

**Total for typical meeting (2-3 external attendees):**
- Quick prep: 5-10 min
- Standard prep: 10-15 min
- Thorough prep: 15-25 min
