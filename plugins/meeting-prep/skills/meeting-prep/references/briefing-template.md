## Section Breakdown

### Header Section

Required elements:
- Meeting title (from calendar)
- Date, time, duration
- Location/video link
- Optional: Purpose if clear from context

### Attendees Section

Per person:
- Name + Title (bold)
- Company | Industry | Size
- LinkedIn URL
- Background bullets (2-4 key points)
- Shared context (mutual connections, past interactions)
- "Key thing to know" one-liner

### Company Section

Include:
- Overview paragraph (what they do)
- Recent developments (news, launches, funding)
- Relevance to meeting purpose

### Context Section

Explain:
- How meeting came about
- What they likely want
- What we want

### Talking Points Section

3-5 actionable points with:
- What to discuss
- Why it matters
- Supporting data if available

### Prep Checklist

Concrete to-dos:
- Documents to review
- Data to prepare
- People to brief
- Technical prep

### Quick Reference

One-line summary table for at-a-glance reminder during meeting.

---

## Formatting Guidelines

**Length:** Single page when printed, ~500-800 words

**Tone:** Scannable, bullet-heavy, bold key facts

**Priority:** Most important info at top of each section

**Links:** Include but don't require clicking (summarize key info)

**Unknowns:** Note explicitly rather than omit ("Limited info available")
