# Lead Response Templates

Response drafts organized by lead tier and situation.

---

## 🔥 Hot Lead Templates

### Standard Hot Response

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out about [specific thing they mentioned].

[Direct answer to their main question if they asked one]

I'd love to learn more about [specific detail from their email]. Are you free for a quick call this week?

Here are a few times that work:
- [Time option 1]
- [Time option 2]
- [Time option 3]

Or grab a time directly: [Calendly link]

Looking forward to connecting.

[Signature]
```

### Hot Response - Has Budget + Timeline

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Great timing on your [Q1/Q2/etc] timeline - we can definitely help with [their specific use case].

Given your [budget/timeline mention], I'd suggest we start with a quick discovery call to map out the implementation. I can have a proposal to you within [timeframe] after we talk.

Free this week? [2-3 time options] or book directly: [Calendly]

[Signature]
```

### Hot Response - Referral

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

[Referrer name] is great - glad they connected us.

From what you mentioned about [their need], this sounds like a strong fit. Happy to share how we helped [Referrer's company or similar company] with [relevant outcome].

When works for a quick call? [Times] or [Calendly link]

[Signature]
```

### Hot Response - Decision Maker

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out directly. Based on what you described, I think we can help [specific outcome they're looking for].

Rather than go back and forth over email, would a 20-minute call work to scope this out? I can come prepared with [relevant examples/case studies/pricing].

A few times: [options] or [Calendly]

[Signature]
```

## 🟡 Warm Lead Templates

### Standard Warm Response

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for your interest in [product/service].

To point you in the right direction, a few quick questions:

1. What's the main problem you're trying to solve?
2. Is there a timeline you're working toward?
3. Who else would be involved in evaluating this?

Happy to jump on a call once I understand your needs better.

[Signature]
```

### Warm Response - Needs More Info

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out!

I'd love to help, but want to make sure I point you to the right resources. Could you tell me a bit more about:

- What you're trying to accomplish?
- Are you evaluating this for a team or personal use?

Once I know more, I can share relevant examples and next steps.

[Signature]
```

### Warm Response - Company Unknown

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for your interest!

I noticed you reached out from a personal email - are you evaluating this for a company? If so, which one?

Just helps me send you the most relevant info.

[Signature]
```

### Warm Response - Specific Question (Not Ready to Buy)

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Great question about [topic they asked about].

[Direct answer to their question]

[Optional: relevant resource link]

If you want to discuss further or see how this would work for your situation, happy to hop on a call. Otherwise, let me know if any other questions come up.

[Signature]
```

## 🧊 Cold Lead Templates

### Standard Cold Response

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out!

Here are some resources that might help:
- [Relevant resource/doc 1]
- [Relevant resource/doc 2]
- [FAQ or getting started guide]

If you have specific questions, just reply to this email.

[Signature]
```

### Cold Response - Student/Personal

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for your interest!

For personal/student use, I'd recommend checking out:
- [Free tier / community version]
- [Documentation / tutorials]

If this is for a company, let me know and I can share more relevant options.

[Signature]
```

### Cold Response - Just Exploring

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Happy to have you exploring!

Here's some info to review at your own pace:
- [Overview / intro content]
- [Use cases / examples]
- [Pricing page if applicable]

When you're ready to talk specifics, just reach out.

[Signature]
```

## Situation-Specific Templates

### Competitor Mention

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Smart to compare options - happy to help.

We often see teams switch from [competitor] because [key differentiator].

Here's a quick comparison: [link if available]

If you want, I can walk you through the specific differences that matter most for [their use case]. Free for a call this week?

[Signature]
```

### Pricing Request (No Other Info)

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Happy to talk pricing!

Our pricing depends on [key variables - team size, usage, features]. To give you an accurate quote, could you share:

1. How many [users/seats/units]?
2. What's the primary use case?
3. Any must-have features?

I can have pricing options to you right after.

[Signature]
```

### Feature Request

```
Subject: Re: [Their subject]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the detailed question about [feature].

[Answer about feature availability / roadmap / workaround]

Is this a critical requirement, or more of a nice-to-have? Helps me understand what would work best for your situation.

[Signature]
```

### Meeting Follow-up (After Initial Call)

```
Subject: Great talking with you about [topic]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the time today. Quick recap:

**What you're looking for:**
- [Key need 1]
- [Key need 2]

**Next steps:**
- [ ] I'll send [deliverable]
- [ ] You'll [their action item]
- [ ] Reconvene on [date]

[Any additional resources discussed]

Let me know if I missed anything.

[Signature]
```

## Template Variables

Replace these placeholders:

| Variable | Replace With |
|----------|--------------|
| `[Name]` | First name from email |
| `[Their subject]` | Keep their subject, add "Re:" |
| `[specific thing]` | What they actually mentioned |
| `[Times]` | 2-3 specific time options |
| `[Calendly link]` | Your scheduling link |
| `[Signature]` | Your email signature |

---

## Tone Guidelines

**Hot leads:** Confident, direct, assume the sale, minimal friction to next step

**Warm leads:** Helpful, qualifying, ask questions before pitching

**Cold leads:** Efficient, provide value, don't over-invest time

**All leads:**
- Keep it short (under 150 words)
- One clear call-to-action
- Reference something specific from their email
- No jargon or buzzwords
